ℹ️ Data Source & Methodology:
• Data from the selected ERA5, ERA5-Ensemble, or ECMWF IFS model via Open-Meteo Historical Weather API. Their native grids are 0.25° (~25 km), 0.5° (~55 km), and ~9 km, respectively.
• Snowfall depth: Open-Meteo daily snowfall sum in centimeters; this is fresh-snow depth, not snow water equivalent.
• Days 1: Count of days where fresh snowfall depth is greater than or equal to the configurable threshold (default: 1.0 cm)
• Days 2: Total calendar days in the queried range
• Snow Frequency: Days 1 ÷ Days 2 (displayed as a percentage and fraction)
• Snowfall SWE Frequency: Days with daily snowfall SWE ≥ 1 mm ÷ total days (displayed as a percentage and fraction)
• ERA5-Ensemble is a separate, 3-hourly ensemble product; ERA5 at 0.25° remains the default
